## Project Overview

This project focused on evaluating the performance of a **Horizontal Subsurface Flow Constructed Wetland (HSSF-CW)** for domestic wastewater treatment.

The study involved a systematic literature review of HSSF constructed wetlands along with experimental evaluation of a pilot-scale system. The system consisted of layered sand and gravel media with **Typha** vegetation and was operated under horizontal subsurface flow conditions.

---

## Objectives

- Evaluate the treatment performance of an HSSF constructed wetland.
- Study the removal of organic matter and suspended solids.
- Analyse changes in important water-quality parameters.
- Study the influence of hydraulic and design parameters on treatment performance.
- Review HSSF-CW applications under different climatic conditions.
- Assess the potential of treated wastewater for agricultural irrigation reuse.

---

## Experimental Setup

- **System:** Horizontal Subsurface Flow Constructed Wetland
- **Dimensions:** Approximately 52 × 40 × 25 cm
- **Flow rate:** Approximately 3.6 mL/min
- **Hydraulic Retention Time:** Approximately 3.5 days
- **Plant:** Typha
- **Media:** Sand and gravel
- **Flow:** Horizontal subsurface flow

---

## Parameters Studied

The system performance was evaluated using:

- Dissolved Oxygen (DO)
- pH
- Total Suspended Solids (TSS)
- Turbidity
- Volatile Suspended Solids (VSS)
- Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD)
- Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)
- Nitrate and Nitrite
- Alkalinity
- Hardness
- Fluoride
- Chloride

---

## Key Results

The experimental study showed significant reductions in several water-quality parameters.

- **TSS:** Reduced from approximately 1000 mg/L to 80–90 mg/L within the first day.
- **Turbidity:** Reduced from approximately 14 NTU to below 2 NTU within the first day.
- **COD:** Reduced from approximately 170 mg/L to around 90 mg/L.
- **BOD:** Reduced from approximately 150 mg/L to around 65 mg/L.
- **pH:** Remained approximately within the range of 6.4–7.0.
- **Nitrogen compounds:** Showed fluctuating behaviour indicating ongoing nitrogen transformation processes.

## Literature Review

A systematic literature review was conducted using the **Scopus database**.

The review considered studies on HSSF constructed wetlands treating domestic or municipal wastewater and compared parameters including:

- Wetland dimensions
- Hydraulic retention time
- Hydraulic loading rate
- Media characteristics
- Plant species
- Pollutant removal efficiencies
- Climatic conditions
- Potential for wastewater reuse

## Irrigation Reuse Potential

The study explored the potential of using treated wastewater for agricultural irrigation.

Although improvements were observed in several water-quality parameters, further testing and comparison with appropriate irrigation-water standards are required before direct application.
